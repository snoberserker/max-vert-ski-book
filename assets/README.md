# Visual provenance

`cover-v2.png` is the full-resolution original cover generated with the built-in image generation tool on November 16, 2026. The final prompt is preserved verbatim in `cover-v2.prompt.txt`. It depicts a fictional skier and alpine landscape; it is photorealistic generated artwork, not documentary photography or a representation of a named mountain.

`cover-v2.webp` is the optimized reading/print-page asset. `cover-v2.jpg` is the social-sharing asset. These are format/encoding derivatives of the original, with no creative alteration. Text: THE LONG SEASON / A FIELD GUIDE TO SKIING / MAX VERT™. Copyright 2026 DidThey LLC.

`cover.svg` is the superseded first cover, retained as source history and no longer used by the renderer. Its original code-drawn geometric landscape was replaced following editorial feedback.

The illustrated edition uses newly generated woodcut-style artwork in petrol, cream and ochre. The cover landscape is fictional. The rope tow, evening hill and bootmaker’s bench are editorial interpretations of chapter subjects. The built-in image generator produced the four images using the prompts in illustrated-edition-prompts.txt. The cover lettering was typeset separately.

The publication directory contains the RGB Kindle front cover and the matching paperback interior and full-wrap cover. The paperback uses 6 × 9 inch trim, premium color on white paper, a 54-page interior without bleed and a 0.126738 inch spine. The cover includes 0.125 inch bleed and space for KDP’s barcode. Regenerate the wrap if the page count or paper changes.
