Use case: ads-marketing.
Create a striking, finished, publication-quality FRONT BOOK COVER for a serious but exhilarating contemporary skiing book. Vertical 2:3 aspect. Flat cover artwork filling the canvas edge to edge, NOT a 3D book mockup, no border, no drop shadow. This should look worthy of an independent outdoor publisher and a beautiful ski magazine.
Title text verbatim: "THE LONG SEASON". Subtitle text verbatim: "A FIELD GUIDE TO SKIING". Small publisher text verbatim: "MAX VERT™".
Art direction: breathtaking documentary-style alpine action photography with tactile fine 35mm film grain, mixed with extremely confident Swiss editorial typography. A single skier, small enough to show the immensity of the mountain but large enough to feel the turn, in the lower middle of the frame, committing to a powerful sweeping powder turn; anatomically credible body, exactly two proper skis, correct poles, beautiful explosive plume of snow, a little vermilion on the skier. Jagged, complex alpine peaks and a vast steep textured snow face behind. Raking winter sunlight, deep blue-black shadows, brilliant snow, cold glacial blue atmosphere. The mountain must feel enormous, raw, beautiful and real. Strong diagonal movement across the frame and genuine spatial depth. NOT a geometrical vector mountain, not a cute travel poster, not flat illustration, not stock vacation advertising.
Typography is central to the design: the words "THE LONG" then "SEASON" in enormous, tightly tracked, bold condensed grotesk uppercase lettering in warm off-white across the upper part of the cover, perfectly legible and meticulously aligned. The mountainous photographic texture should remain visible around the lettering. Create a strong relationship between title and alpine ridge; intentional cropped photographic composition, sophisticated negative space. Small subtitle near the bottom with excellent legibility over deep shadow. Small publisher mark at top. One thin restrained vermilion rule or editorial accent if it improves the composition.
The result should convey speed, altitude, obsession, winter culture and the pleasure of skiing. Premium collectible ski publication, beautifully printed photographic cover, confident and uncompromising. Avoid extra text, slogans, badges, watermarks, rounded fonts, gradients-as-background, clip art, simplistic polygon mountains, fake aged distress, crowds, ski lifts. Exact spelling of all three specified texts.
