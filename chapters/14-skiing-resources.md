A useful skiing library has more than opinions about equipment. It includes current weather, official operations information, education, history, and people you can ask for help. This directory explains what each resource is for and where its limits begin.

Links were checked for this edition on November 16, 2026. Linked services can change, and some content or tools may require payment. Listing a resource is not an endorsement of every claim or product on its website. No paid placement is included in this edition.

## History and understanding the sport

**[International Skiing History Association](https://www.skiinghistory.org/):** A specialist starting point for ski history, equipment development, biographies, and archival reading. Some material requires membership. Begin with [A Short History of Skis](https://www.skiinghistory.org/history/short-history-skis-0) and [Evolution of Ski Shape](https://www.skiinghistory.org/history/evolution-ski-shape).

**[National Ski Areas Association industry statistics](https://www.nsaa.org/NSAA/About/Media/Industry_Stats/NSAA/About/Media/Industry_Stats.aspx):** U.S. industry measures and links to historical series. Check the year, definition, and whether a number is preliminary or final. The [press-release index](https://www.nsaa.org/NSAA/About/Media/Press_Releases/NSAA/About/Media/Press_Releases.aspx?hkey=5daf59b4-830b-4b1d-8d93-bb11b3800c50) helps locate updates. The final 2025/26 PDF linked there was unavailable during preparation; the industry chapter uses an explicitly dated accessible benchmark.

**[Vail Resorts investor relations](https://investors.vailresorts.com/):** Primary company disclosures about its own business. Company explanations are a perspective on the business, not an independent account of the entire industry.

## Learning and participation

**[PSIA-AASI / The Snow Pros](https://thesnowpros.org/):** U.S. snowsports instructor education and certification. Use local ski schools to book appropriate instruction and ask about instructor qualifications.

**[Move United](https://moveunitedsport.org/):** Adaptive sport programs and participation resources. Contact the relevant local program to confirm skiing disciplines, equipment, availability, and individual support needs.

**Your local ski club or mountain school:** Often the best source for repeatable learning opportunities, group logistics, and community. Confirm program details directly; this book does not maintain a live directory of every club.

## Conditions and trip decisions

**[National Weather Service winter safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/winter):** U.S. winter hazards and links to forecast information. Use the relevant official weather agency outside the United States.

**[NOAA / Drought.gov snow drought](https://www.drought.gov/what-is-drought/snow-drought):** An explanation of snow drought and why warmth and precipitation both matter to snowpack. Useful background for interpreting a winter beyond a single snowfall headline.

**[Max Vert snow desk](/data/) and [resort forecasts](/data/resorts/):** Starting points for exploring mountain conditions. Follow through to current resort operations and official forecasts before making travel or skiing decisions.

**[Max Vert road desk](/data/roads/), [fares](/data/flights/), and [stays](/data/stays/):** Trip research starting points. Confirm closures and traction requirements with transport authorities and current prices and terms with the provider.

**The mountain’s official website:** The source to consult for operating terrain, lift status, access rules, reservations, lessons, and closures. Weather aggregators cannot establish that a particular lift will run.

## Health and safety

**[AAOS skiing injury prevention](https://www.orthoinfo.org/staying-healthy/sports-injury-prevention/skiing-injury-prevention/):** General preparation and injury-prevention education. Your clinician should guide individual medical questions and return after injury.

**[CDC adult activity guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html):** A general activity baseline. These recommendations are not an individualized ski training plan.

**[CDC altitude guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html):** Acclimatization, altitude illness, and travel considerations. Ask a clinician about personal risk when planning a high-altitude trip.

**[CDC HEADS UP concussion symptoms](https://www.cdc.gov/heads-up/signs-symptoms/index.html):** Signs, symptoms, and emergency danger signs. Suspected concussion ends the ski session and requires appropriate assessment.

**[NSAA Your Responsibility Code](https://www.nsaa.org/NSAA/Safety/Responsibility_Code/NSAA/Safety/Your_Responsibility_Code.aspx?hkey=341ee8dd-2dba-4255-b689-6105e62352d7):** The full U.S. slope code. Also learn the actual rules and signs where you ski.

**[Avalanche.org](https://avalanche.org/):** U.S. regional avalanche forecasts and education connections. Use the local avalanche service for your destination. Reading a forecast or an online introduction does not qualify you for independent avalanche terrain travel.

## How to use a source well

Before acting, ask who produced the information, what place and time it covers, and whether it answers your actual question. A season-level industry number does not predict Saturday’s queue. A ten-day forecast does not authorize a road trip. A list of exercises does not clear an injured knee.

Keep a small set of dependable resources rather than a huge collection of bookmarks you never read. For a familiar mountain, that may be the official operations page, official weather, the road authority, patrol information, and your instructor or shop.

## About this edition

*The Long Season* is a free web book published by DidThey LLC under the MAX VERT™ name. It focuses on recreational lift-served skiing, primarily from a North American planning perspective, with broader historical and travel context. It is general education, not individualized medical care, professional instruction, or backcountry certification.
